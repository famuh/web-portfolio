Overview 🚀


I am a Mobile Developer with over 2 years of experience building and enhancing applications for Android, tablets, and Android TV using Flutter. At DNA Group (DMB Division), I developed a Self-Service Ordering System for Android TV and tablet devices, maintained existing applications to ensure stability and performance, and managed application updates on the Google Play Store. I also contributed to developing key features such as facial recognition and fake GPS detection for attendance, offline mode for POS, notification system, and multi-outlet management.

In addition, I served as a Flutter Facilitator in the BEKUP (Baparekraf for Startup) program, where I facilitated weekly sessions, monitored participant progress, supported instructor-led trainings, and guided learners through technical workshops and collaborative programming strategies.

I am also experienced in integrating API and BaaS (Backend as a Service) using Firebase and Supabase, including authentication, real-time databases, cloud storage, and API management, to deliver seamless and scalable applications.

Currently, I am expanding my expertise in web development using React, complementing my strong foundation in mobile development, UI/UX design, and system integration. With this combination of skills, I am well-prepared to deliver robust, scalable, and user-centered digital solutions.
